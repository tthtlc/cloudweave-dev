
These questions are a **security posture map** for a CMP: each one tests whether identity, authorization, secrets, exposure, and auditability are controlled, observable, and recoverable. In practice, answering them helps you classify controls into preventive posture, detective posture, governance/integrity posture, and resilience/incident-response posture. [dexidp](https://dexidp.io/docs/configuration/tokens/)

## How to use them

The fastest way to use this list in a CMP is to tag each answer as one of four outcomes: **healthy**, **misconfigured**, **suspicious**, or **missing visibility**. Questions that land in “misconfigured” weaken preventive posture, questions that land in “missing visibility” weaken detective/forensic posture, and questions that land in “suspicious” should trigger targeted incident review across Dex, backend, OpenFGA, Vault, and network exposure points. [github](https://github.com/hashicorp/web-unified-docs/blob/main/content/vault/v1.7.x/content/docs/audit/index.mdx)

A good operational pattern is to convert each question into a dashboard tile plus one alert rule plus one investigation query. For example, q1/q10/q26 become trust-boundary checks, q12/q18/q19/q20 become authz-integrity checks, and q6/q21/q32 become secret-audit and evidence-quality checks. [tag-security.cncf](https://tag-security.cncf.io/community/assessments/projects/openfga/joint-assessment/)
